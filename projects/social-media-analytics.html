Project Accenture Data Analytics-Python

12-02-2022 / Irene Y.

1. Project Understanding

1.1 Client Background

Over the past 5 years, the client has reached over 500 million active users each month. They have scaled quicker than anticipated and need the help of an advisory firm to oversee their scaling process effectively. Due to their rapid growth and digital nature of their core product, the amount of data that they create, collect and must analyze is huge. Every day over 100,000 pieces of content, ranging from text, images, videos and GIFs are posted. All of this data is highly unstructured and requires extremely sophisticated and expensive technology to manage and maintain.

There are 3 main reasons why they are now looking at bringing in external expertise:

  • They are looking to complete an IPO by the end of next year and need guidance to ensure that this goes smoothly.
  • They are still a small company and do not have the resources to manage the scale that they are currently at. They could hire more people, but they want an experienced practice to help instead.
  • They want to learn data best practices from a large corporation.

1.2 Tasks to be Delegated

  • Creation of an up-to-date big data best practices presentation
  • Extraction of sample data sets using SQL
  • On-site audit of their data-center
  • Merging of sample data set tables
  • Loading of sample data sets into Accenture sandbox database
  • Technology architecture workshop with Data Team to understand their technology landscape
  • Stress testing of their technology to identify weak spots
  • Communication with previous IPO companies within client base for reference stories
  • Analysis of sample data sets with visualizations

2. Data Cleaning & Modeling

2.1 Requirements Gathering

The key data tables include User, Profile, Location, Session, Content, Reaction, and ReactionTypes — each with specific fields such as User ID, Content ID, Reaction Type, Sentiment, and Score.

2.2 Data Cleaning

The data model connects users to their content and reactions. Key fields include:

  • Content: Content ID, User ID, Type, Category, URL
  • Reaction: Content ID, User ID, Type, Datetime
  • ReactionTypes: Type, Sentiment (positive/negative/neutral), Score (popularity quantifier)

Data Source: Forage — Accenture Data Analytics Virtual Experience

2.3 Data Modeling

Merged and cleaned datasets to create a unified analysis-ready table linking content categories with reaction scores and sentiment.

3. Data Visualization & Storytelling

3.1 Data Visualization

Used the cleaned data set to create insightful visualizations to address the requirements of the project.

3.2 Create a Presentation

Ensured that the presentation of the results is clear and can be easily understood by the internal team and the client.

3.3 Storytelling

Incorporated storytelling techniques throughout the presentation, making it as engaging and persuasive as possible to communicate data-driven content strategy recommendations.
