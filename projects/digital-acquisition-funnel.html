Digital Acquisition Funnel Analysis

Marketing Analytics · Camp Australia · 2023–2024

📌 Overview

Analysed 1M+ digital interactions via Google Analytics to map the full acquisition funnel, identify drop-off points, and optimise conversion performance across paid and organic channels for Camp Australia's customer acquisition strategy.

🏆 Key Contributions

  • Mapped the end-to-end acquisition funnel from awareness (ad impressions, organic search) through to account creation and first booking.
  • Identified critical drop-off points where 40%+ of potential customers abandoned the registration process.
  • Built attribution models comparing last-touch, first-touch, and multi-touch attribution across paid search, social, and organic channels.
  • Delivered recommendations to marketing and product teams, resulting in UX improvements to the sign-up flow.
  • Created automated reporting tracking weekly funnel metrics and channel-level conversion rates.

🛠 Tech Stack

Google Analytics SQL Funnel Analysis Python Attribution Modelling Power BI

📈 Outcome

★ Improved acquisition funnel conversion through data-driven optimisation. Recommendations led to measurable improvements in registration completion rates and more efficient allocation of marketing spend across channels.
