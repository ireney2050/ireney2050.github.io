📊 Published Research: Child Impact Survey (2023)

In collaboration with Camp Australia coworkers, I led the development and publication of the 2023 Child Impact Survey, which was featured in The Sector. My insights on child wellbeing were featured in an ABC News interview with Camp Australia CEO Warren Jacobson ABC NEWS.

🔍 Overview

The survey captured insights from over 3,000 families and educators across Australia, shedding light on the emotional, social, and developmental impacts of the COVID-19 pandemic on children. It provided a data-driven foundation for understanding how children are adapting post-pandemic and informed sector-wide discussions on education and care.

🏆 Key Contributions

  • Designed and executed a national survey strategy targeting diverse family and educator demographics.
  • Analyzed and synthesized large-scale qualitative and quantitative data to identify key trends and challenges.
  • Published findings in a widely respected industry outlet, influencing policy discussions and educational practices.
  • Promoted awareness of children's wellbeing in the post-COVID landscape through accessible, actionable insights.
