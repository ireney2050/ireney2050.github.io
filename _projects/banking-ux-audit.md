## Overview

Conducted comprehensive UX and technology trend audits across 100+ banks worldwide at iSky Research, a global financial benchmarking firm serving top-tier institutions including Chase (US) and Citi (HK).

## The Business Problem

Leading banks needed to understand how their digital products compared to global competitors. Without structured benchmarking data, product teams lacked the competitive intelligence needed to prioritise their digital roadmaps and investment decisions.

## What I Did

- Audited digital banking experiences across 100+ banks spanning multiple geographies and product lines
- Designed performance frameworks evaluating 200+ digital features for usability, functionality, and innovation
- Identified emerging UX and technology patterns to inform strategic product roadmaps
- Authored 300+ expert blog posts on UX benchmarks and customer feedback trends

## Tools & Technologies

UX Auditing Frameworks, Competitive Benchmarking, Feature Scoring, Content Strategy

## Key Outcomes

- Delivered competitive intelligence used by top-tier banks to shape digital strategy
- 300+ published analyses, increasing platform readership by **20%+**
- Performance frameworks adopted as standard evaluation tools across client engagements
