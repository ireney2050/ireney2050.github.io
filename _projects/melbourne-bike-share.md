## Overview

Applied linear programming in Python to optimise bike distribution across Melbourne's public bike-share system during an Accenture consulting internship, addressing a real operational challenge for the City of Melbourne.

## The Business Problem

Melbourne Bike Share (MBS) operates 50+ docking stations and 600+ bikes across the inner city. During peak commuting hours, bikes accumulate at popular destinations while origin stations run empty — creating a rebalancing problem that degrades user experience and reduces system utilisation.

## What I Did

- Modelled the bike rebalancing problem as a linear optimisation problem
- Built the optimisation model in Python to determine optimal bike redistribution across 50+ stations
- Incorporated constraints including station capacity, demand patterns, and redistribution logistics
- Presented the solution and methodology to the consulting team

## Tools & Technologies

Python, Linear Programming (SciPy/PuLP), Mathematical Optimisation, Data Analysis

## Key Outcomes

- Produced an optimised redistribution plan that minimised unmet demand during peak hours
- Demonstrated the application of mathematical optimisation to a real urban logistics problem
- Presented findings as part of the Accenture consulting engagement
