## Overview

Developed predictive forecasting models to anticipate booking demand across Camp Australia's 500+ school sites, enabling the operations team to optimise staffing levels and capacity planning.

## The Business Problem

Camp Australia manages before-school, after-school, and holiday care programs across hundreds of locations. Understaffing leads to service quality issues and compliance risk; overstaffing erodes margins. The operations team needed accurate demand forecasts to make staffing decisions days or weeks in advance.

## What I Did

- Analysed booking patterns across 75,000+ customers and 4.5M+ annual bookings using SQL and Python
- Engineered features from historical booking data, school calendar events, seasonality, and site-level trends
- Built and evaluated multiple forecasting approaches to predict demand at the site level
- Delivered forecasts integrated into operational planning workflows

## Tools & Technologies

Python (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn), SQL (Snowflake), Time Series Analysis, Feature Engineering

## Key Outcomes

- Improved demand prediction accuracy by **20%+** over previous methods
- Enabled optimised staffing levels, reducing both under- and over-staffing
- Forecasts were adopted into the operational planning process across the national network

> **Note:** This project used proprietary Camp Australia data. Code and datasets cannot be shared publicly, but the methodology and approach are detailed above.
