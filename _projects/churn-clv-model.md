## Overview

Built customer lifetime value (CLV) estimation and churn propensity models for Camp Australia's base of 75,000+ customers, enabling the executive team to prioritise retention efforts and allocate resources more effectively.

## The Business Problem

With a large and diverse customer base spanning 500+ schools, Camp Australia needed to move beyond reactive customer management. The business lacked a systematic way to identify which customers were at risk of churning and which segments delivered the highest lifetime value.

## What I Did

- Designed cohort segmentation framework classifying customers as new, retained, or lapsed based on booking behaviour patterns
- Built churn propensity models using historical booking data across 4.5M+ annual transactions
- Estimated customer lifetime value by segment to quantify the revenue impact of retention vs. acquisition
- Delivered findings to executive stakeholders, translating model outputs into actionable retention strategies

## Tools & Technologies

Python (Pandas, Scikit-learn), SQL (Snowflake), Cohort Analysis, Logistic Regression, Feature Engineering

## Key Outcomes

- Provided the executive team with a data-driven framework for retention planning
- Identified high-risk customer segments before churn events, enabling proactive intervention
- CLV estimates informed budget allocation decisions between acquisition and retention spend

> **Note:** This project used proprietary Camp Australia data. Code and datasets cannot be shared publicly, but the methodology and approach are detailed above.
