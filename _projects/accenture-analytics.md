## Overview

Analysed content engagement data for a social media platform with 500M+ monthly active users as part of the Accenture Data Analytics Virtual Experience on Forage. Identified top-performing content categories and delivered an executive-ready presentation.

## The Business Problem

The client, a rapidly scaling social media company, was producing over 100,000 pieces of content daily but lacked clarity on which content categories drove the most engagement. With an IPO on the horizon, they needed data-driven content strategy recommendations.

## What I Did

- Cleaned and merged multiple datasets (user profiles, content metadata, reactions, session data) into a unified analysis-ready structure
- Identified top content categories by engagement volume and reaction sentiment
- Built visualisations communicating content performance trends clearly
- Created a presentation translating analytical findings into strategic recommendations

## Tools & Technologies

Python (Pandas, Matplotlib), Data Cleaning, Exploratory Data Analysis, Presentation Design

## Key Outcomes

- Identified the top 5 content categories driving the majority of positive engagement
- Delivered a clear, executive-ready presentation with actionable content strategy recommendations
- Demonstrated end-to-end analytics workflow from raw data to business insight
