## Overview

Led the end-to-end development and publication of the 2023 Child Impact Survey at Camp Australia, Australia's largest Out of School Hours Care (OSHC) provider. The survey captured insights from over 3,000 families and educators nationwide, providing a data-driven view of how children were adapting in the post-COVID landscape.

## The Business Problem

Camp Australia needed to understand the evolving needs of families and the emotional, social, and developmental impacts of the pandemic on children across their network. The organisation also required credible, publishable research to strengthen its brand authority and inform strategic direction.

## What I Did

- Designed and executed a national survey strategy targeting diverse family and educator demographics across 500+ school communities
- Analysed large-scale qualitative and quantitative responses to identify key trends and challenges
- Synthesised findings into actionable insights for the executive team
- Led a separate NPS survey of 5,000+ families, measuring satisfaction and identifying drivers of loyalty and churn

## Tools & Technologies

Python, Survey Design, Statistical Analysis, NPS Methodology, Data Storytelling, Stakeholder Presentation

## Key Outcomes

- Published findings in *The Sector*, a widely respected industry outlet, influencing policy discussions and educational practices
- Insights were featured in an **ABC News interview** with Camp Australia CEO Warren Jacobson
- NPS survey results directly shaped organisational strategy and resource prioritisation
- Established Camp Australia as a thought leader in child wellbeing research
