## Overview

Designed and delivered 100+ advanced Power BI dashboards for Camp Australia, integrated with Snowflake, providing data-driven visibility across state, school, and customer segment performance for Australia's largest OSHC provider.

## The Business Problem

Camp Australia's leadership, regional managers, and school-level coordinators all needed different views of performance data — from high-level national KPIs to granular school-by-school booking trends. The existing reporting was fragmented, manual, and unable to keep pace with the organisation's scale.

## What I Did

- Designed a comprehensive Power BI reporting suite covering operational, financial, and customer performance metrics
- Built data models in Snowflake and Power BI connecting booking, customer, staffing, and financial data
- Pioneered the **"Traffic Lights Priority" reporting framework** for 500+ schools, colour-coding performance against targets to streamline management attention
- Created self-service dashboards enabling regional managers to drill into their own data

## Tools & Technologies

Power BI, Snowflake, SQL, DAX, Data Modelling, Stakeholder Requirements Gathering

## Key Outcomes

- Delivered **100+ dashboards** used across all levels of the organisation
- Traffic Lights framework became the standard performance tracking tool across 500+ schools
- Reduced manual reporting effort and enabled faster, more consistent decision-making
- Dashboards covered state-level strategy, school-level operations, and customer segment analytics
