## Overview

Analysed 1M+ digital interactions via Google Analytics to map Camp Australia's full customer acquisition funnel, identifying conversion bottlenecks and optimising performance across paid and organic channels.

## The Business Problem

Camp Australia's digital marketing team was investing across multiple channels (search, social, email, direct) but lacked a unified view of how traffic converted into bookings. Without funnel-level visibility, the team couldn't identify where potential customers were dropping off or which channels delivered the best return.

## What I Did

- Extracted and structured 1M+ session and event-level records from Google Analytics
- Mapped the complete acquisition funnel from first visit through to booking confirmation
- Identified key drop-off points at each funnel stage, segmented by channel, device, and geography
- Presented findings with recommendations for conversion rate optimisation

## Tools & Technologies

Google Analytics, SQL, Python (Pandas), Funnel Analysis, Channel Attribution

## Key Outcomes

- Provided the first complete funnel-level view of digital acquisition performance
- Identified high-impact drop-off points that informed UX and marketing improvements
- Enabled the marketing team to reallocate spend toward higher-converting channels

> **Note:** This project used proprietary Camp Australia data. Specifics are anonymised, but the methodology is detailed above.
